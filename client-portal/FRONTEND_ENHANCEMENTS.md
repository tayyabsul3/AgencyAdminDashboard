## 📋 Complete Feature List

### **Immediately Available:**
✅ **FAQ Selection** - Choose which FAQ to generate video for  
✅ **Duration Control** - 5-30 seconds slider  
✅ **Quality Toggle** - SD (720p) or HD (1080p)  
✅ **Emotion Selection** - 5 voice emotions with emojis  
✅ **Caption Toggle** - Enable subtitles for accessibility  

### **Advanced Options:**
✅ **Avatar Picker** - 100+ avatars with gender labels  
✅ **Voice Picker** - 300+ voices with language tags  
✅ **Speed Control** - 0.5x to 1.5x with slider  
✅ **Pitch Control** - -50 to +50 with slider  

### **Smart Features:**
✅ **Auto-load on expand** - Avatars/voices fetch when advanced panel opens  
✅ **Loading states** - Shows "(Loading...)" while fetching  
✅ **Visual feedback** - Selected quality button highlights  
✅ **Real-time updates** - Speed/pitch values show live

## 🎯 How Users Will Use It

### **Simple Mode (Default)**
1. Select FAQ from dropdown
2. Adjust duration slider (5-30s)
3. Choose quality (SD/HD)
4. Pick emotion (Friendly, Excited, etc.)
5. Toggle captions on/off
6. Click "Generate Video"

**Perfect for:** Quick video generation with sensible defaults

### **Pro Mode (Advanced Options)**
1. Do all Simple Mode steps
2. Click "▼ Advanced Options"
3. Browse 100+ avatars
4. Browse 300+ voices by language
5. Fine-tune speed (0.5x-1.5x)
6. Fine-tune pitch (-50 to +50)
7. Click "Generate Video"

**Perfect for:** Content creators who want full control
